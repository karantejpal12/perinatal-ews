perinatal-ews

Early-warning analytics for county maternal and newborn health. It flags US counties where preterm birth, low birth weight, late or no prenatal care and gestational hypertension are getting worse than their own recent history predicts, checks its own reliability, forecasts next-year risk, and shows where warning signs meet high overdose burden or thin access to treatment and delivery care.

Who it is for

  • State and county health department epidemiologists
  • Maternal and child health programmes and perinatal quality collaboratives
  • Researchers working with natality data

What it does

  • Detects county deterioration against each county's own baseline, with empirical-Bayes shrinkage for small counties, national-trend adjustment and false-discovery control.
  • Looks inside Medicaid and privately insured births, where a change can be hidden in the county total.
  • Separates state-wide patterns, which may reflect policy or recording changes, from local ones.
  • Adds county context: drug overdose deaths, opioid treatment for pregnant and postpartum patients, hospitals with labour and delivery care, and adult health and social conditions, from public CDC, SAMHSA and CMS data. A machine-learning model compares each county with counties in similar conditions.
  • Groups counties into types with unsupervised clustering, checks the input data with an anomaly-detection model, and gives 90% prediction ranges (conformal prediction) for the comparison with similar counties.
  • Answers questions through an AI assistant that looks up the published data with the tool's own functions, shows each lookup and checks every figure in its answer against the data.
  • Forecasts next-year risk with a machine-learning model validated forward in time against simpler baselines.
  • Monitors itself: data quality, distribution drift, backtested alert rates and performance by county size.

Run it yourself

With Python 3.10 or newer:

pip install git+https://github.com/karantejpal12/perinatal-ews
perinatal-ews demo --out demo        # synthetic demo, opens demo/report.html
perinatal-ews run --input your_data.csv --out results --forecast

Instructions for downloading county data from CDC WONDER are in the README.
