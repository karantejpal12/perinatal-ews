Run perinatal-ews on your own data

The full tool runs inside your browser. Your file never leaves your computer: nothing is uploaded to any server. The first run downloads the Python engine (about 30 MB, once), which takes a minute.

1. Choose data

A CSV in the perinatal-ews format, one row per county, year, indicator and subgroup: geo_id, geo_name, state, year, indicator, subgroup, numerator, denominator. Use All as the subgroup for county totals. Indicators: preterm_birth, low_birth_weight, late_no_prenatal_care, gestational_hypertension. At least 6 consecutive years.
